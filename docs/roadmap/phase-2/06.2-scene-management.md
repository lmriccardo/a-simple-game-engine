# Scene Management — Phase 2

*Priority: Tier 2 follow-on. See [README](../README.md). Other phases:
[Phase 1](../phase-1/06.1-scene-management.md) (load/save),
[Phase 3](../phase-3/06.3-scene-management.md) (world streaming).*

## Goals
Reusable entity templates (prefabs), and loading more than one scene at a time
(additive loading — e.g. a persistent HUD scene layered over a level scene).

## Design notes
- **Prefabs are a template over [Phase 1](../phase-1/06.1-scene-management.md)'s scene
  format** — the same entity+component+asset-reference shape, instantiated multiple
  times with per-instance overrides, rather than a separate concept.
- **Additive loading** needs scenes to be independently load/unload-able without
  tearing down the whole `Registry` — depends on Phase 1's load/save being scoped to
  a subset of entities, not "the entire game state". The foundation for this landed
  ahead of schedule in Phase 1's `SceneManager`: every entity is tagged with a
  `SceneId`, all resident scenes share one `Registry`, and `EvictCachedScene`/
  `UnloadScene` already destroy just one scene's entities. What's still missing here
  is *simultaneous* activity — `SceneManager::ActiveEntities()` scopes to exactly one
  `SceneId`, not a set, so today only one resident scene is ever actually processed
  at a time (see `examples/scene_demo`'s `MoveActiveEntities`/`RenderActiveEntities`).
  Additive loading is that restriction lifted, not new plumbing underneath it.
