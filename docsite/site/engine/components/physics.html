Physics components

Collision shapes and motion. See the Physics theory page for how they are used.

Collider

A rectangle or circle, offset from the entity's Transform. A Solid collider is pushed out of what it overlaps; a Trigger never blocks and fires signals instead. Layer and mask decide who can collide with whom.

TOML table: [Collider] · Used by: CollisionSystem · Source: Game/Components/Collider.hpp

FieldDefaultMeaning
m_LocalBoundsdefault RectA Rect or Circle plus a local offset.
m_ResolutionSolidSolid (push-out), Trigger (sensor) or Unknown (ignored).
m_Layer1Bit(s) this collider occupies.
m_Maskall bitsLayers it is willing to collide with.
m_OnEnter / m_OnStay / m_OnExitlazy signalsPer-entity trigger signals, called with the other entity.

Rigidbody

Makes an entity respond to physics.

TOML table: [Rigidbody] · Used by: PhysicsSystem · Source: Game/Components/Rigidbody.hpp

FieldDefaultMeaning
m_Mass1Mass.
m_AffectedByGravitytrueWhether gravity accelerates it.

Velocity

Linear velocity in world units per second.

TOML table: [Velocity] · Used by: MovementSystem, PhysicsSystem · Source: Game/Components/Velocity.hpp

FieldDefaultMeaning
m_DX0Change in X per second.
m_DY0Change in Y per second.
