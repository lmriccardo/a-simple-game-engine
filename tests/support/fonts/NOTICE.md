# Ahem.ttf

Source: https://github.com/web-platform-tests/wpt/blob/master/fonts/Ahem.ttf

The Ahem font belongs to the public domain. In jurisdictions that do not
recognize public domain ownership, it is additionally available under the
Creative Commons Zero (CC0) declaration:
http://labs.creativecommons.org/licenses/zero-waive/1.0/us/legalcode

Ahem is maintained by the W3C (http://www.w3.org) specifically for use in
automated rendering tests: every glyph is a solid black square filling its
character cell (with a few deliberate exceptions for specific test cases),
which makes rendered output pixel-predictable -- exactly what's needed here
to assert DrawText actually draws the glyphs it claims to, not just that the
calls don't crash.
